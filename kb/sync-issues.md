# Tasks Not Saving or Syncing

If tasks or comments fail to save, or changes made on one device do not
appear on another, walk the customer through, in order:

1. Refresh the page (or force-close and reopen the mobile app).
2. Check status.flowdesk.app for an ongoing incident.
3. Sign out and back in.
4. Clear the browser cache, or reinstall the mobile app.

If the problem persists after all four steps, collect the browser or app
version and an example task ID, and escalate to engineering as a bug report.
