# Upgrading or Downgrading a Plan

Customers change plans themselves under Settings > Billing > Change Plan.
Upgrades take effect immediately, with a prorated charge for the remainder
of the cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date; no partial
refunds are issued for downgrades mid-cycle.

Team plans require at least 3 seats. Removing seats below 3 automatically
converts the account to a Pro plan at renewal.
