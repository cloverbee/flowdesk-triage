# Active Service Incidents

Use this article only when an engineering alert is currently firing for the
part of Flowdesk the customer is having trouble with — for example, a
sync-service alert for tasks or comments not saving or syncing.

1. Tell the customer: "This is a known issue that our engineering team is
   already working on."
2. Point them to status.flowdesk.app for live updates.
3. Do not ask them to work through troubleshooting steps, and do not promise
   a time for the fix.

The incident alone does not need an escalation — engineering already has it.
Still escalate if the ticket also involves lost data, a legal threat, a
security concern, or an extremely upset customer.

If no alert is firing, ignore this article and follow the normal article for
the customer's issue (for example, Tasks Not Saving or Syncing).
