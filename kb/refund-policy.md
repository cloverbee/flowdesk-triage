# Refund Policy

Monthly plans: refundable in full within 14 days of the charge, no questions
asked. After 14 days, no refund; the plan simply does not renew if cancelled.

Annual plans: refundable within 30 days of the charge. After 30 days,
refunds are prorated and require manager approval — escalate these.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business
days to appear.
