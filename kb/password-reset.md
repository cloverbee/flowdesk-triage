# Resetting Your Password

Customers can reset their own password from the sign-in page using the
"Forgot password" link. A reset email arrives within 5 minutes; it expires
after 1 hour. If the email does not arrive, ask the customer to check spam
and confirm which address their account uses.

Support agents cannot set passwords manually. If the reset email repeatedly
fails to arrive at a confirmed correct address, escalate to the accounts team.
